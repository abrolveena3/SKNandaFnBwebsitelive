The Nanda Legacy · Established 1997

A Family Legacy.
Three Stories, One Standard of Excellence.

Founded by Mr. S.K. Nanda and propelled into modern luxury gastronomy by Pratik and Manan—combining ancestral culinary reverence, operational mastery, and avant-garde experiential dining.

All Three Founders Mr. S.K. Nanda Pratik Nanda Manan Nanda

Mr. S.K. Nanda

Founder & Managing Director

Founded in 1997 · 28+ Years of Culinary Heritage

28+ Years Heritage Royal Indian Gastronomy Purity of Ingredients

In 1997, Mr. S.K. Nanda laid the foundation of SK Nanda Catering with an unshakeable conviction: that unforgettable celebrations are born from uncompromising culinary purity, personal integrity, and authentic Indian hospitality.

Rooted in the timeless traditions of slow-fire Awadhi dum cooking, artisanal Punjabi tandoor, and royal North Indian feasts, Mr. Nanda spent decades perfecting proprietary spice blends and sourcing relationships with premier organic farms and heritage spice purveyors. Under his personal oversight, what began as a bespoke boutique catering house evolved into one of North India's most respected culinary institutions, trusted by leading corporate dynasties, royal families, and high-profile wedding hosts across the nation.

"Hospitality is an act of pure devotion. When a host entrusts you with their family's celebration, every single plate must carry the genuine warmth and dignity of a home."

— Mr. Surinder Kumar Nanda

Pratik Nanda

Director & Chief Executive Officer

Executive Leadership & Global Operations

Global Scale Logistics Destination Weddings ISO Culinary Standards

Stepping forward to guide SK Nanda into modern global gastronomy, Pratik Nanda pairs classical culinary reverence with state-of-the-art operational mastery. Recognizing that luxury celebrations demand seamless execution under extreme conditions, he transformed the logistics of large-scale Indian banqueting.

Pratik engineered mobile temperature-controlled field kitchens, precision-timed multi-course service choreography, and destination wedding execution capabilities across India's royal palaces, coastal resorts, and international venues. Under his executive leadership as CEO, SK Nanda delivers events of unprecedented scale—ensuring that multi-thousand guest galas receive the same warmth, temperature, and bespoke artisanal finesse as an intimate chef's table.

"True luxury at scale is invisible precision. Whether we are serving three hundred guests or seven thousand, the consistency of taste, temperature, and grace must never waver."

— Pratik Nanda

Manan Nanda

Director & Head of Experience Design

Culinary Curation & Theatrical Dining

22 Live Station Theatre Haute Plating & Decor Experiential Menus

As Director, Manan Nanda infuses artistic innovation and contemporary experiential dining into every celebration. Believing that modern guests eat with their eyes and imagination first, Manan has redefined how banquet cuisine is presented and enjoyed.

He is the creative architect behind SK Nanda's celebrated 22 interactive live stations—including the Takashi Sushi Vitrine, 24-Month Parmigiano Reggiano Wheel Pasta Bar, and theatrical Teppanyaki griddles. Collaborating closely with host families, luxury event planners, and international guest chefs, Manan crafts bespoke gastronomic narratives tailored to each family's unique heritage and palate.

"A banquet is not just a meal; it is an immersive theatrical performance where visual drama, exotic aromas, and heartfelt taste converge into memories that last a lifetime."

— Manan Nanda

Planning your celebration

Questions, answered with precision.

Do you offer fixed catering packages?

No. Every menu is tailor-made around the occasion, guest profile, culinary preferences and service format.

Which locations do you serve?

We serve Delhi NCR, events across India, and selected international celebrations with end-to-end planning and on-ground execution.

Can the menu accommodate dietary preferences?

Yes. Dietary requirements, regional preferences and the pace of service are mapped before the menu is finalised.

Which event formats do you cater?

Weddings and pre-wedding celebrations, corporate events, social occasions and international events are all supported.

Is a tasting included in the planning process?

Tasting is one of the six planning stages, allowing dishes to be refined before the final event execution.

Is the online estimate the final price?

No. It is an indicative range only. Final pricing is bespoke after consultation and can exclude venue, décor and outstation travel.
